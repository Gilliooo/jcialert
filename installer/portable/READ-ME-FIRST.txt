JCIAlert 1.0.1 - Indonesian market news alerts
==============================================

TO INSTALL
    Double-click  SETUP.bat

    Installs into your own user profile
    (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\JCIAlert). No admin rights, no UAC prompt.
    Adds a Start Menu shortcut and starts the app.

TO REMOVE
    Double-click  REMOVE.bat        keeps your settings and history
                  REMOVE.bat /purge deletes those too


THREE THINGS THAT SURPRISE PEOPLE
---------------------------------

1. NO TRAY ICON? Look under the ^ chevron in the tray first.
   Windows 11 hides new tray icons there by default. Drag it out to pin it.
   This is the commonest "it didn't work" and it always did.

2. THE FIRST RUN IS QUIET ON PURPOSE.
   Each news source's current backlog is recorded and NOT alerted, so you
   don't get a hundred hours-old headlines at startup. Alerts begin with
   the next story published after you start it.

3. COPY THE WHOLE FOLDER, not just JCIAlert.exe.
   emiten.json is 250 KB of ticker data and is deliberately not bundled
   into the exe, so it can be refreshed without a rebuild. The exe on its
   own cannot start.


IF IT STILL DOESN'T APPEAR
--------------------------
    "%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\JCIAlert\JCIAlert.exe" --doctor

It checks every reason it can fail to start - missing files, a folder it
can't write to, a broken config, a second copy already running - and says
which one, in a dialog. Also available from the tray: right-click ->
Diagnostics...


WINDOWS SMARTSCREEN
-------------------
It will warn the first time: "Windows protected your PC".
Click "More info" -> "Run anyway". The app isn't code-signed; that needs a
certificate, and nothing in the build can work around it.


WHAT IT DOES
------------
Watches Indonesian financial news feeds and pops up when a headline names
a ticker you care about. Right-click the tray icon:

    News dashboard...   every headline it has seen, and why each did or
                        did not alert. Also the source health tab.
    Options...          watchlist, filters, sources, popup corner
    Check now           poll immediately
    Diagnostics...      why it isn't working

Settings live in config.json next to the program. The watchlist is the
main one: EMPTY MEANS EVERY TICKER, not none.
